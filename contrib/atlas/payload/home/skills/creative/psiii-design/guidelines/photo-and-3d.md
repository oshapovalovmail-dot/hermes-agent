# Photography & 3D icons

The brandbook defines imagery through **AI-generation prompts** rather than a
stock library. Reproduce these directions; don't hand-draw or use off-brand stock.

## Photo direction 1 — dramatic editorial portrait
Heroic studio portrait, **chiaroscuro** directional light, deep dramatic shadows
emphasizing facial structure. **Rim light** in vibrant orange / deep red separating
the subject from an intensely lit background. Keep the subject's exact likeness;
treat the lighting as a filter over the original. Photorealistic, highly detailed.
Background near-black `#260102`.

## Photo direction 2 — warm everyday (base presentation photos)
Warm sunny palette dominates: light yellow, peach, soft orange, cream; deep rich
accents (dark blue, burgundy, emerald) for contrast; cool light-blue/gray-blue in
the background for balance. Natural, slightly muted "live" colors, smooth
transitions. Cinematic, naturalistic; medium/close-up with air around the subject;
soft background focus; natural daylight, soft warm diffused shadows; slight
asymmetry. Effects: light grain / vintage texture, soft edge vignette, gentle sky
gradients, muted HDR, selective saturation on key subjects. Mood: **warm, cozy,
slightly dreamy** — calm morning daylight, harmony and tranquility.

## 3D icons
High-end clean **3D product render**: keep the line-icon's silhouette, add a soft
internal warm glow (amber / orange), gentle rim light and soft reflections on a
clean light-gray / off-white base with minimal warm glowing accents. Ultra clean,
no noise, no visible construction, futuristic premium feel. Rendered on a **black
background**. Use sparingly — section openers, hero moments.

> Both photo styles share a **warm** bias and high craft. Never cool, never noisy,
> never flat stock. Pair imagery with white slides and ink type.
